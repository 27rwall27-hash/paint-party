10 players, 5 rounds. Each round, before the rocks are struck, you'll see the exact gleam to watch for. Hold the mouse button down to raise your pickaxe, then release the instant that gleam flashes on it. Every pickaxe flashes the same sequence of shapes and colors at once — some real, most decoys — and the target's spot in the sequence changes every round, so you actually have to watch. Release at the wrong moment (or never release at all) and your rock stays standing. The 4 fastest correct swings earn Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Verdite ore.
