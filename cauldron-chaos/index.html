5 rounds. Each round the cauldron flashes a list of potion ingredients — fast, and it only gets faster. Then, taking turns, walk to the shelf, pick up a bottle, and carry it to the cauldron. The same potion can sit on the shelf more than once — pour a copy of something that was on the list and it's safe, as long as a copy is still left. Pour wrong, or once every copy's been used, and it backfires — you're out for the round. Last one still brewing scores the most.
