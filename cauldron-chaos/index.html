5 rounds. 10 potions sit on the shelf, each a different color. Each round the cauldron flashes a list of 12 colors — fast, and it only gets faster. Then, taking turns, walk to the shelf, pick up a bottle, and carry it to the cauldron. Every bottle refills the instant it's taken, so the shelf always looks full — but each color is only safe to pour as many times as it showed up on the list. Pour a color that's used up its safe count, or one that was never on the list, and it backfires — you're out for the round. Last one still brewing scores the most.
